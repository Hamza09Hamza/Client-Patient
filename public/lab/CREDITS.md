## A note before going live

Stock photography of someone else's laboratory on a clinic's own sign-in page
is a judgement call the clinic should make deliberately, not inherit from a
placeholder — patients may reasonably read these as pictures of Clinique
Amina. The three Unsplash frames should be replaced with the clinic's own
photographs when those exist.
